#  My Favorite K-Drama Series

Why I like it

it has an interesting love story,
beautiful characters
exciting fantasy scenes. 
I love the chemistry between Jung Gu-won and Do Do-hee
because they make the story entertaining and romantic.
I also enjoy the combination of comedy, action, and emotional moments.


Top 3 things I love 
The romantic relationship between the main characters.
The beautiful cinematography.
The interesting supernatural storyline.
The amazing acting of Song Kang and Kim Yoo-jung.
